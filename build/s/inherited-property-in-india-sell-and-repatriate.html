Papers to gather

  • Title deed and chain of earlier deeds
    Buyers and banks check the full chain. Where: Your records; sub-registrar.
  • Purchase cost proof: original sale deed value, payment records, improvement bills
    Capital gains (and TDS certificate) are worked out from it. Where: Your records, builder receipts.
  • Death certificate, will/probate or succession/legal heir papers, and mutation in your name
    Needed before you can sell. Where: Municipality, court, revenue office.
  • PAN, passport, OCI card, overseas address proof
    Required for the deed, TDS and bank. Where: Your documents.
  • Buyer's TDS payment proof and TDS certificate
    Needed for your tax return and repatriation. Where: From the buyer; income-tax portal.
  • CA certificate and online declaration for the remittance (Forms 15CA/15CB or new equivalents)
    Banks need it before sending money abroad. Where: Your chartered accountant; bank.
  • NRO statement showing sale proceeds and the bank's remittance form
    Banks track repatriation limits. Where: Your bank.
  • Indian income-tax return for the year of sale
    Claims refund of excess TDS and shows tax compliance. Where: Income-tax portal / CA.

You may also need, depending on your answers

  • Release / relinquishment deeds or consent of other heirs — A sale without all co-owners is risky for buyers.
  • Apply for a lower/nil TDS certificate (through a CA) before the sale — Avoids TDS on the full sale value.
  • Sale PoA executed abroad (consulate, or notary + apostille), stamped in India — Registrars reject improperly executed PoAs.
  • Ask your bank to redesignate old resident accounts as NRO — FEMA requires resident accounts to be redesignated when you become NRI; frozen accounts are a common result.

What the law says

Buyers must deduct tax (TDS) from payments to NRI sellers on the full sale value unless a lower/nil deduction certificate is obtained. From 1 April 2026 these rules sit in the Income-tax Act, 2025 (renumbered sections and forms). Repatriation is governed by FEMA and RBI rules.
