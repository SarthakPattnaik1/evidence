Where you stand

  • In your favour Mutation does not decide ownership — a wrong entry does not by itself take away your title.
  • Next step An RTI and a written reminder to the Tehsildar often move a stuck mutation.

Do these now

  • File an RTI for the status and file notings of your application
    Often moves a stuck file. Where: State RTI portal or by post.

Papers to gather

  • The document that gives you the property (sale deed, gift/partition deed, will, or allotment)
    Mutation follows the title document. Where: Your records; certified copy from the Sub-Registrar.
  • Current record extract (7/12, khatauni, jamabandi, patta, khata or property tax record)
    Shows the entry to be changed. Where: State land records portal or Tehsil / municipal office.
  • Land revenue or property tax receipts
    Supports possession and the correction. Where: Your records.
  • Your mutation application, acknowledgement and any rejection order
    Needed for appeal or follow-up. Where: Tehsil / municipal portal.
  • ID proof of all applicants
    Asked in every application. Where: Aadhaar / voter ID.

What the law says

Land and property records are kept by the state revenue department (rural) or the municipal body (urban) under state land revenue laws. Mutation is ordered by the Tehsildar / Revenue Officer after notice to everyone affected; appeals lie to higher revenue officers. Courts have repeatedly held that mutation entries do not create or end ownership — title depends on documents such as a sale deed, will, partition or inheritance. Many states now give a time limit for mutation and let you apply and track online.
