Your situation

  • What is happening? Images are already posted or shared

Where you stand

  • In your favour Platforms must remove intimate or morphed images within 2 hours of your complaint.
  • Next step If a platform ignores you, its Grievance Officer and then the Grievance Appellate Committee are the next steps.

Do these now

  • Report to the platform (choose "nudity / non-consensual / impersonation")
    They must act within 2 hours for this kind of content. Where: Report button in the app.
  • Complaint on cybercrime.gov.in or at the police station
    Starts investigation and lets police order takedowns. Where: cybercrime.gov.in (you can report anonymously in this section)
  • Use StopNCII.org to block the image on partner platforms
    Works without sending the actual image. Where: stopncii.org

Papers to gather

  • Links (URLs) and usernames where it is posted or sent from
    Platforms remove by link; police trace accounts by username. Where: Copy from the app (Share → Copy link).
  • Screenshots of threats and demands, with date and time
    Proves the crime even if they delete it. Where: Your chats.

What the law says

IT Rules (amended, in force from 20 February 2026): platforms must remove non-consensual intimate images, including AI-made or morphed ones, within 2 hours of a complaint. Sharing such images is a crime under the IT Act and the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita. Report on cybercrime.gov.in (women and child safety section) or 1930.
