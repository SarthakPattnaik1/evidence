Road to my land blocked by others

What usually matters in this situation, and the papers to keep ready before you meet an advocate or free legal aid (call 15100).

Your situation

  • What is happening? My access/path is blocked

Papers to gather

  • Title deed (sale deed, gift deed, partition deed, patta) — certified copy
    The foundation of any claim. Where: Your records; certified copy from sub-registrar.
  • Revenue records: record of rights, khatian/7-12/jamabandi, mutation entries
    Shows record changes. Where: State land records portal, tehsil.
  • Survey map, layout plan, sketch with boundaries and measurements
    Needed for boundary disputes. Where: Survey office, layout approval.
  • Land revenue / property tax receipts and utility bills
    Supports possession. Where: Municipality, tehsil.
  • Dated photos/videos of the land before and after the encroachment
    Shows the encroachment. Where: Your phone; neighbours.
  • Names of neighbours who know the boundaries and your possession
    Supporting witnesses. Where: —

You may also need, depending on your answers

  • Copies of police/revenue complaints and replies — Shows you acted in time.

What the law says

Civil courts decide title and injunctions; revenue authorities handle measurement, demarcation and records. Long, open possession by someone else can create claims, so do not delay.
