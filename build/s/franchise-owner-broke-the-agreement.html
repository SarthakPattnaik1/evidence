Franchise owner broke the agreement

What usually matters in this situation, and the papers to keep ready before you meet an advocate or free legal aid (call 15100).

Your situation

  • What kind of deal? Franchise / distribution

Papers to gather

  • Signed contract with annexures, amendments and SOWs
    The foundation. Where: Your files.
  • Delivery records, milestones, acceptance and rejection notes
    Central to breach. Where: Project records, email.
  • Payment records and invoices
    Shows the financial position. Where: Accounts.

You may also need, depending on your answers

  • Emails, quotations, POs and messages forming the deal — A contract can be formed by correspondence.
  • Notices of breach, termination and replies — Fixes timeline and cure periods.
  • Loss calculation with supporting bills, lost orders, replacement costs — Courts and tribunals award proven loss.
  • Bank guarantee and invocation letter — Urgent injunctions turn on exact wording.
  • NDA/confidentiality clauses and proof of misuse — Supports injunction claims.
  • Steps you took to limit the loss — Required for damages.

What the law says

Commercial disputes of ₹3 lakh or more go to commercial courts, generally after mandatory pre-institution mediation unless urgent interim relief is needed. If the contract has an arbitration clause, courts usually refer the dispute to arbitration but can grant interim protection.
