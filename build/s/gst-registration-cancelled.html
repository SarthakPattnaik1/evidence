Papers to gather

  • The notice with its DIN, section and year
    Check it is genuine (DIN) and which law applies. Where: e-filing portal → e-Proceedings / GST portal.
  • Your return for that year and its acknowledgement
    The notice compares against it. Where: Portal.
  • AIS / Form 26AS (income tax) or GSTR-1, 3B, 2B (GST)
    Most notices come from mismatches. Where: Portal.
  • Proof for the items questioned (bank statements, bills, sale deeds, invoices)
    Replies need documents, not just words. Where: Your records.

  • Reply online before the deadline (or ask for more time on the portal) — Silence leads to an ex parte order.

What the law says

The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026; notices for earlier years may still be under the 1961 Act. Under the new Act, reassessment notices are issued under Sections 280–281 (earlier 148/148A), and scrutiny follows a notice served within set time limits. All notices carry a DIN and can be seen and answered on the e-filing portal (e-Proceedings). For GST, demands for FY 2024-25 onwards are under Section 74A of the CGST Act (earlier Sections 73/74), and paying within 60 days of the notice reduces the penalty.
