Neighbour harasses and abuses my family

What usually matters in this situation, and the papers to keep ready before you meet an advocate or free legal aid (call 15100).

Your situation

  • What is the problem? Abuse, threats or harassment

Papers to gather

  • Your sale deed, allotment letter or rent agreement
    Every office first asks who you are. Where: Your property file / landlord.
  • Dated photos and videos of the problem, taken over several days
    Repeated proof is stronger than one photo. Where: Your phone.
  • Messages, recordings or witness names for abuse or threats
    Needed for a police complaint. Where: Phone; neighbours who saw it.
  • Copies of every complaint you made and any reply
    Courts and magistrates ask what you tried first. Where: Society, municipality, police acknowledgments.

You may also need, depending on your answers

  • Written complaint to the society / RWA — Societies can act on common areas and leakage between flats.
  • Message to your landlord asking them to act — Some remedies must be pursued by the owner.

What the law says

Unauthorised construction is handled by the municipal or development authority under state building laws. A public nuisance can be stopped by an order of the Sub-Divisional / Executive Magistrate under Section 152 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (earlier Section 133 CrPC). A civil court can grant an injunction and damages for private nuisance or interference with light, air or a common wall. Noise is governed by the Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules, 2000 — loudspeakers are generally not allowed between 10 pm and 6 am.
