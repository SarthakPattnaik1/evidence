What kind of payment was it?
Why: The reversal deadline depends on the payment type.		UPI to a person; UPI to a shop or website; Card at a shop machine; Card online; ATM — cash not given; IMPS / net banking transfer
How long ago was it?
Why: Tells us whether the reversal deadline has passed.		Today or yesterday; 2 to 5 days; 6 to 30 days; More than 30 days
What does your app, slip or statement show?
Why: A "success" status needs proof the other side did not get the money.		Failed; Pending; Successful, but the other side says they did not get it; I'm not sure

Why: A complaint reference is needed before going to RBI.		Yes, with a reference number; Not yet
