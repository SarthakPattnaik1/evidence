Selling or inheriting property in India and moving the money abroad

Please check every statement below against the current law and practice. Write corrections in the last column. Saboot is a free preparation tool: it lists papers and facts to take to an advocate and never recommends an advocate.

1. What the law says (shown to users)

Buyers must deduct tax (TDS) from payments to NRI sellers on the full sale value unless a lower/nil deduction certificate is obtained. From 1 April 2026 these rules sit in the Income-tax Act, 2025 (renumbered sections and forms). Repatriation is governed by FEMA and RBI rules.

  • Reserve Bank of India — FEMA rules for NRIs
  • Income Tax e-filing portal (official) — the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from 1 Apr 2026

3. Papers and steps listed

Paper / stepWhy / where / timeCorrection
Title deed and chain of earlier deeds (some cases)Buyers and banks check the full chain. Where: Your records; sub-registrar.
Purchase cost proof: original sale deed value, payment records, improvement bills (some cases)Capital gains (and TDS certificate) are worked out from it. Where: Your records, builder receipts.
Death certificate, will/probate or succession/legal heir papers, and mutation in your name (some cases)Needed before you can sell. Where: Municipality, court, revenue office.
Release / relinquishment deeds or consent of other heirs (some cases)A sale without all co-owners is risky for buyers. Where: Sub-registrar (registered deeds).
Step: Apply for a lower/nil TDS certificate (through a CA) before the sale (some cases)Avoids TDS on the full sale value. Where: Income-tax portal (a CA usually files it). Time: Apply well before the sale — processing takes time.
PAN, passport, OCI card, overseas address proofRequired for the deed, TDS and bank. Where: Your documents.
Sale PoA executed abroad (consulate, or notary + apostille), stamped in India (some cases)Registrars reject improperly executed PoAs. Where: Documents signed abroad for use in India are usually executed before an Indian embassy/consulate, or before a local notary and then apostilled (for Hague Apostille countries). Time: Documents executed abroad generally must be stamped within 3 months of reaching India — check your state's stamp law.
Buyer's TDS payment proof and TDS certificate (some cases)Needed for your tax return and repatriation. Where: From the buyer; income-tax portal.
CA certificate and online declaration for the remittance (Forms 15CA/15CB or new equivalents)Banks need it before sending money abroad. Where: Your chartered accountant; bank.
NRO statement showing sale proceeds and the bank's remittance formBanks track repatriation limits. Where: Your bank.
Step: Ask your bank to redesignate old resident accounts as NRO (some cases)FEMA requires resident accounts to be redesignated when you become NRI; frozen accounts are a common result. Where: Your bank.
Indian income-tax return for the year of sale (some cases)Claims refund of excess TDS and shows tax compliance. Where: Income-tax portal / CA.
