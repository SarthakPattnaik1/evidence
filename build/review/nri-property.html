My property in India is occupied, misused or claimed while I live abroad

Please check every statement below against the current law and practice. Write corrections in the last column. Saboot is a free preparation tool: it lists papers and facts to take to an advocate and never recommends an advocate.

1. What the law says (shown to users)

NRIs and OCIs can own and inherit residential and commercial property in India. They generally cannot buy agricultural land, plantations or farmhouses, but can inherit them. You can act through an advocate and a power-of-attorney holder without living in India.

  • Specific Relief Act, 1963
  • Limitation Act, 1963
  • MADAD consular grievance portal (MEA)
