Why: Employees, agency contract workers and freelancers use different routes.		Directly employed; Through a contractor or agency; Daily wage; Freelancer / consultant; I'm not sure
Are you still working there?
Why: Resignation or termination records then become important.		Still working; I resigned; I was removed; The business has closed or disappeared

Why: Tells us which records prove the amount.		One month; 2 to 3 months; More than 3 months; Only final settlement / dues on leaving

3. Papers and steps listed

Paper / stepWhy / where / timeCorrection
Appointment letter, offer email or contract (some cases)States the salary and terms. Where: Your email or files.
Salary slips (some cases)Shows the agreed salary and any PF/tax deducted. Where: HR portal, email.
Bank statement showing past salary credits from the employer (some cases)Shows the employment and the usual amount. Where: Net banking.
EPFO (UAN) passbook or ESI records (some cases)Employer contributions are independent proof of employment. Where: EPFO member portal / UMANG app; ESI portal.
Employee ID card, visiting card or uniform (some cases)Supporting proof of employment. Where: Your belongings.
Attendance, timesheets, rosters, work emails or work-group messagesShows work was done for the months claimed. Where: Work email, WhatsApp work groups, attendance app.
Records of cash wages (notebook entries, messages, co-workers' names) (some cases)Cash wages need indirect proof. Where: Your notes, messages.
Anything linking you to the employer (photos at work, delivery records, customer messages, co-workers) (some cases)Without formal papers, several small pieces of proof can work together. Where: Your phone, messages.
Your written request for salary and the employer's replies (some cases)Shows the employer knew and did not pay. Where: Email, WhatsApp.
Resignation, termination letter or relieving letter (some cases)Final settlement dues depend on the exit date. Where: Email or HR portal.
Employer's registered name and address (and GST/CIN if known)Needed for any notice or complaint. Where: Offer letter, salary slip, invoices, GST portal search, MCA company search.
Details of the contractor/agency and the principal company where you worked (some cases)Both may have obligations to pay. Where: Gate pass, agency letters.
Your invoices, work orders and payment history (freelancers) (some cases)Freelance claims are usually contract claims. Where: Email, invoices.
