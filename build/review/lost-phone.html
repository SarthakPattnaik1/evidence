My phone was stolen or lost, or I lost important documents

Please check every statement below against the current law and practice. Write corrections in the last column. Saboot is a free preparation tool: it lists papers and facts to take to an advocate and never recommends an advocate.

1. What the law says (shown to users)

Theft is an offence under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023; most state police let you file an e-FIR for theft or a lost-article report online. The Department of Telecommunications’ CEIR system (on Sanchar Saathi) blocks a phone on all networks by IMEI and helps trace it, using your police report number. Duplicate documents are issued by the department that issued them, usually against the police report.

  • Sanchar Saathi — block or trace a lost phone (CEIR)
  • Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
  • National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal
