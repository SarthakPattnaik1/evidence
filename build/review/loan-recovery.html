Loan or EMI trouble — recovery calls, bank notices, settlement

Please check every statement below against the current law and practice. Write corrections in the last column. Saboot is a free preparation tool: it lists papers and facts to take to an advocate and never recommends an advocate.

1. What the law says (shown to users)

RBI rules govern recovery conduct and digital lending. Secured-loan enforcement follows the SARFAESI Act, with appeals to the Debts Recovery Tribunal.

  • RBI complaint portal (Integrated Ombudsman Scheme)
  • RBI (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025 — 8 May 2025

What kind of loan is it?
Why: Home, vehicle and other secured loans can lead to property being taken; unsecured loans cannot, without a court.		Home or property loan; Vehicle loan; Personal loan or credit line; Loan from a mobile app; Business / MSME loan; Gold loan

Why: Banks and RBI-registered NBFCs are covered by RBI complaint routes; unregistered lenders are not.		A bank; A finance company (NBFC); An app, not sure who is behind it; A private moneylender; I'm not sure

Why: Each problem has its own protections and records. Why: Loans overdue for more than 90 days are usually classified as non-performing, which triggers formal recovery.		Not behind; 1–2 EMIs; 3 or more EMIs; Loan already closed; I'm not sure

Where: From the lender or agent; photos of the notice on the property. Time: Challenges to SARFAESI possession are usually due within 45 days at the DRT.
Settlement offer or sanction letter on the lender's letterhead (some cases)Verbal settlements are a common trap. Where: Ask the lender in writing.
Closure proof and your written request for NOC and original documents (some cases)RBI rules require original property documents to be returned within 30 days of closure, with compensation for delay. Where: Final payment receipt, closure letter.
