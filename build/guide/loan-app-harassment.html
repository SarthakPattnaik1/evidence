What the law says

RBI's Digital Lending Directions, 2025 require regulated lenders to list their apps in RBI's public directory, disclose all charges in a Key Fact Statement, tell you about recovery agents in advance, and stay away from your contacts and photos. Threats and morphed images are crimes and can be reported to police and 1930.

Papers that usually help

  • App name, developer, and screenshots of the loan screen
    Identifies the app and any bank/NBFC behind it.
  • Call recordings, messages and the numbers used
    Main proof of threats and abuse.
  • Messages your contacts received (from them)
    Shows misuse of your data.
  • The morphed images or threat to post them
    Needed for police; platforms can remove posted images.
  • Amount received and every repayment made
    Shows unfair charges and what (if anything) is owed.
  • Key Fact Statement and loan agreement
    Anything not disclosed cannot be charged.
  • Police / 1930 complaint about threats
    Threats and morphed images are criminal.
  • Report the app on RBI Sachet
    For apps not linked to a regulated lender.

Questions Saboot will ask you

  • Have you complained anywhere?
