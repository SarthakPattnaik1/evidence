Papers that usually help

  • Transaction reference (UTR / RRN), date, time and amount
    The bank tracks failed payments by this reference.
  • Debit SMS and the statement entry
    Shows the money left your account.
  • Screenshot of the failed or pending screen
    Shows the status at the time.
  • ATM slip, ATM ID and location, and exact time
    The bank checks the ATM's records against your claim.
  • Written confirmation from the shop or person that they did not receive it
    Strong support where your app shows "success".
  • Bank complaint reference and all replies
    Needed for an RBI complaint.
  • Raise a written complaint with your bank and keep the reference
    Starts the formal process and the record for compensation.
  • RBI complaint (cms.rbi.org.in) and its number
    Available when the bank rejects or does not resolve within 30 days.
