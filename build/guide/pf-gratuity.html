PF not deposited, PF withdrawal stuck, or gratuity not paid

PF problems usually go to EPFO; unpaid gratuity goes to the Controlling Authority. Both are free and need no lawyer to start.

What the law says

The Code on Social Security, 2020 (in force from 21 November 2025) now covers both. Gratuity is due after 5 years of continuous service (1 year for fixed-term employees), capped at ₹20 lakh, and must be paid within 30 days of becoming due. The EPF wage ceiling was raised to ₹25,000 a month from 17 September 2026.

Papers that usually help

  • Salary slips showing PF deductions
    Proves the employer took your share.
  • EPF passbook and UAN details
    Gaps show non-deposit.
  • Employer name, establishment code and your dates of joining and leaving
    EPFO tracks deposits by establishment.
  • EPS scheme certificate or pension payment order (PPO)
    Needed for pension disputes.
  • Claim rejection message and reason
    Most rejections are KYC or date mismatches that can be fixed.
  • Aadhaar, PAN and bank details matching EPFO records
    Name, date of birth and bank mismatches block claims.
  • Appointment letter, relieving letter and last salary details
    Gratuity is calculated from them.
  • Written gratuity claim to employer and any reply
    Needed before the Controlling Authority.
  • File on EPFiGMS
    Gets EPFO to act on deposit, claim or transfer issues.
