I lent money and it has not been returned

Proof of payment alone may not prove it was a loan. We will look for anything showing the person agreed to repay.

What the law says

Time limits apply to money claims. A written acknowledgement or part-payment can affect them. Your advocate will check.

Papers that usually help

  • Transfer records (statement, UPI history, cheque encashment)
    Proves payment and the date.
  • Proof of where the cash came from (withdrawal slip or statement) and when it was handed over
    Supports a cash loan.
  • Signed agreement or promissory note
    The clearest proof of a loan.
  • Complete chat where they accept the debt or promise to repay
    Shows the money was a loan and may affect time limits.
  • Records of any part-payments they made
    Part-payment supports the loan and may affect time limits.
  • The original cheque and the bank's return memo
    Needed for a cheque-bounce case.
  • The cheque (check its date)
    Cheques are generally valid for 3 months from their date.
  • Names and contact details of people present
    Can support an oral loan.
  • Your written demand and any reply
    Their response may admit or dispute the debt.
  • Messages around the time of the payment (them asking for money, saying why they needed it, or promising to return it)
    Without a signed paper, the surrounding messages often show the money was a loan and not a gift.
  • A dated list of every amount given and every amount repaid
    Your advocate needs the exact balance claimed.
  • Ask your advocate whether to send a written demand or a legal notice
    A demand and the reply (or silence) can matter later.
  • Borrower's full name and current address
    Needed for a notice or a case.

Questions Saboot will ask you

  • How was the money given?
  • Is there anything in writing showing it was a loan?
  • When was the money given?
  • Since then, has the person paid anything back or admitted the debt?
  • Did they give you a cheque for repayment?
  • When did you get the bank's cheque return memo?
  • Was anyone present when the money was given or discussed?
  • Have you asked for repayment in writing?
