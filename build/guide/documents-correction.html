What the law says

Name change for adults is usually done by an affidavit, a newspaper notice and publication in the Gazette (Department of Publication for central, or the state gazette). Birth records are corrected by the Registrar under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969; since 1 October 2023, a birth certificate is the single document to prove date of birth for births after that date, and delayed registration needs an order. Aadhaar allows name and date-of-birth changes only a limited number of times. A pending criminal case can lead to passport refusal, but a court can permit one.

Papers that usually help

  • Affidavit stating old and new name/detail, on stamp paper, notarised
    First step for name change and most corrections.
  • Your birth certificate or school leaving / 10th certificate
    Other records are corrected to match it.
  • All IDs showing the mismatch (Aadhaar, PAN, passport, certificates)
    You will update each one.
  • Newspaper notice of the name change
    Needed for gazette publication.
  • Gazette notification of the new name
    Accepted by passport, PAN and banks.
  • Marriage certificate or divorce decree
    Often enough for a surname change.
  • Hospital birth record or other old evidence of birth
    Needed for late registration or DOB correction.
  • Details of any pending criminal case and court permission
    A court can allow a passport despite a pending case.
