What the law says

Cheating is an offence under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita; online fraud is reported on 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in. Unregistered deposit schemes can also be reported on RBI's Sachet portal.

Papers that usually help

  • All chats, group names, links and the app or website used
    Shows the false promises and identifies the operators.
  • All payments made and any small "returns" received
    Receiving accounts are frozen using these details.
  • Job offer, "appointment letter" or scheme brochure
    Proves the promise was false.
  • Complaint acknowledgement number or FIR
    Needed for bank and court steps.
  • Names and contacts of other victims
    Supports a joint complaint.
  • Report on 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in
    Starts the freeze process.
  • Report the scheme on RBI Sachet
    For unregistered deposit or chit schemes.
