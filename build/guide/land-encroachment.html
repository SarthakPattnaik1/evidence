Someone is encroaching on or claiming my land

Land disputes are decided on title papers, revenue records and who is in possession. Speed matters.

Answer a few questions and get your list →

What the law says

Civil courts decide title and injunctions; revenue authorities handle measurement, demarcation and records. Long, open possession by someone else can create claims, so do not delay.

Papers that usually help

  • Title deed (sale deed, gift deed, partition deed, patta) — certified copy
    The foundation of any claim.
  • Earlier chain of title deeds (previous owners)
    Answers rival claims.
  • Revenue records: record of rights, khatian/7-12/jamabandi, mutation entries
    Shows record changes.
  • Survey map, layout plan, sketch with boundaries and measurements
    Needed for boundary disputes.
  • Demarcation report / apply for official measurement
    Independent measurement settles many disputes.
  • Land revenue / property tax receipts and utility bills
    Supports possession.
  • Dated photos/videos of the land before and after the encroachment
    Shows the encroachment.
  • Encumbrance certificate
    Detects fraudulent sales.
  • Copies of police/revenue complaints and replies
    Shows you acted in time.
  • Names of neighbours who know the boundaries and your possession
    Supporting witnesses.
