What the law says

Trademarks, copyright and designs are protected by statute; unregistered brands can still be protected through passing off. Post-employment non-compete clauses are generally unenforceable in India, but confidentiality obligations can be enforced.

Papers that usually help

  • Trademark/design registration certificate or application
    Basis of infringement action.
  • Proof of earliest and continuous use: invoices, ads, packaging, dated web pages
    Decides priority and passing off.
  • Sales figures and marketing spend
    Needed for passing off.
  • Dated screenshots, URLs, test purchases and invoices of the copy
    Shows what is copied and where.
  • Instances of customer confusion (messages, reviews, misdirected orders)
    Strong evidence in brand cases.
  • Original files with creation dates (source files, drafts, code repos)
    Proves authorship in copyright cases.
  • Employment/consultancy agreement with confidentiality clauses; exit records
    Basis for action over secrets.
  • Evidence the data or clients were taken (access logs, client messages)
    Supports injunction.
