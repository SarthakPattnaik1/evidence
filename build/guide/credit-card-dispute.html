What the law says

RBI's credit card directions set rules on billing, closure, charges and recovery. For card fraud from 1 Jan 2027, the bank must give a temporary ("shadow") reversal within 5 calendar days of your complaint, and you bear nothing for third-party fraud reported within 5 calendar days.

Papers that usually help

  • Card statements for the months involved
    Shows exactly what was billed.
  • SMS/email alerts for the disputed transactions
    Fixes timing for liability.
  • Report the fraud to the issuer now, block the card, and keep the reference
    Your liability depends on how fast you report.
  • Card block confirmation and the bank's complaint reference
    Limits further loss and fixes the reporting time.
  • Cybercrime complaint number (1930 / cybercrime.gov.in)
    Helps trace and hold the money; banks often ask for it.
  • Your closure request (email, app request, letter) and acknowledgement
    The 7-working-day rule runs from this date.
  • Anything showing what you did or didn't consent to (application form, recorded calls, OTP messages)
    Unsolicited cards and upgrades need your explicit consent.
  • Most Important Terms and Conditions (MITC) and fee schedule
    Charges not disclosed may not be payable.
  • Order, cancellation and merchant correspondence
    Supports a chargeback request.
  • Recovery call logs and messages
    Abusive recovery breaches RBI rules.
  • Your credit report showing the wrong entry
    Needed for the correction request.
  • Issuer's reply or proof of no reply
    Needed for an RBI Ombudsman complaint.
