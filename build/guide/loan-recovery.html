What the law says

RBI rules govern recovery conduct and digital lending. Secured-loan enforcement follows the SARFAESI Act, with appeals to the Debts Recovery Tribunal.

Papers that usually help

  • Loan agreement, sanction letter and key fact statement (KFS)
    Shows what you actually agreed to.
  • Loan account statement (statement of account)
    Shows EMIs paid, charges and the outstanding.
  • Your bank statement showing EMI debits and bounces
    Checks the lender's figures.
  • Call log, recordings and messages from recovery agents
    Abusive conduct breaches RBI rules.
  • Messages your family/friends received from the lender
    Contacting your contacts to shame you is a serious breach.
  • The notice received, with envelope
    Each notice has its own reply/objection period.
  • Possession notice, panchnama or vehicle repossession papers and inventory
    Possession must follow procedure; vehicle seizure needs notice and an inventory.
  • Settlement offer or sanction letter on the lender's letterhead
    Verbal settlements are a common trap.
  • Closure proof and your written request for NOC and original documents
    RBI rules require original property documents to be returned within 30 days of closure, with compensation for delay.
  • Your complaint to the lender's grievance officer and reply
    Usually required before an RBI Ombudsman complaint.
  • App name, download link, screenshots of loan terms, and permissions it took
    Needed for police and RBI complaints.
  • Proof of hardship (termination letter, medical bills, business accounts)
    Supports a restructuring or settlement request.
  • Insurance policy linked to the loan
    May pay EMIs in some situations.
