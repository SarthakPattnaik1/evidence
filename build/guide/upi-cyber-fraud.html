What the law says

Until 31 Dec 2026: for an unauthorised transaction caused by a third-party breach, you bear nothing if you tell your bank within 3 working days. For transactions from 1 Jan 2027 (RBI directions of 24 June 2026): the window is 5 calendar days, the rules also cover payments you were tricked or pressured into making, banks must decide within 45 days, and small frauds (loss up to ₹50,000) can get a one-time compensation of 85% of the net loss or ₹25,000, whichever is lower — only if you report to BOTH your bank and 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in within 5 calendar days.

Papers that usually help

  • Report at 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in and keep the acknowledgement number
    An official report can lead to the receiving account being put on hold.
  • 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in acknowledgement number
    Your advocate and the police will use it to track the complaint.
  • Transaction records for every payment (date, time, amount, UTR/reference)
    The 12-digit UTR or reference is the key to tracing each payment.
  • Bank SMS and email alerts for those payments
    Alert times help show when you first knew.
  • Your written complaint to the bank and its reference number
    For money taken without your action, the time you informed the bank can affect whether you bear any loss.
  • The complete conversation with the scammer (chat, call log, emails, links)
    Shows what was promised or claimed, and who was involved.
  • Anything left about the scammer (numbers, names, website, payment request)
    Even partial details help.
  • Receiving UPI ID, account number or phone number
    Lets the police and banks act on the receiving account.
  • Name of the app you were asked to install, with screenshots
    Helps the investigation and your bank claim.
  • Police complaint or FIR copy
    Often needed for bank claims and recovery.
  • The bank's reply (refund, rejection or none)
    Needed to escalate to the RBI complaint system.
  • The fake ad, job offer, website or investment page
    Shows the deception.

Questions Saboot will ask you

  • When did the money leave your account?
  • Which best describes what happened?
  • Where have you reported it so far?
  • How did the money go?
  • Do you still have the messages, call log, links or app the scammer used?
  • Do you know where the money went (UPI ID, account number or phone)?
  • Is the app the scammer asked you to install still on your phone?
  • What has the bank said?
