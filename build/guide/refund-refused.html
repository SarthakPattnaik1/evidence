What the law says

Consumer complaints generally must be filed within two years of the problem arising. Forum depends on the amount claimed.

Papers that usually help

  • Invoice, bill or order confirmation
    The basic proof of the purchase.
  • Payment proof
    Shows the amount and date.
  • Cash bill or receipt
    Cash purchases need a bill.
  • The ad, listing, or terms as shown when you paid
    Shows what you were told.
  • Refund, return or cancellation policy
    Sellers are expected to follow their stated policy.
  • Photos or videos of the defect (and unboxing video if any)
    Direct proof of the problem.
  • Tracking history and delivery status
    Shows non-delivery or late delivery.
  • Cancellation confirmation
    Shows the refund became due.
  • Job sheet, service report or booking details
    Shows the service failure.
  • Warranty card or warranty terms
    Warranty claims depend on the period and terms.
  • All complaint tickets, emails and replies
    The refusal is usually the key document.
  • Make a written complaint to the seller and keep the reply
    Creates a dated record.
  • National Consumer Helpline docket number
    Shows you tried to resolve it.
  • Card dispute / chargeback request to your bank
    An additional route for card payments.
