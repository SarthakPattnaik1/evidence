What the law says

FEMA rules require accounts held as a resident to be redesignated as NRO once you become non-resident. Indian tax notices from 1 April 2026 are issued under the Income-tax Act, 2025 (older years may still refer to the 1961 Act).

Papers that usually help

  • Bank's freeze/KYC message and account statements
    Shows the reason.
  • Passport, visa/residence permit, overseas address proof, PAN, photo (attested as the bank asks)
    Standard NRI KYC set.
  • Written request to the bank to redesignate/unfreeze, with reference number
    Starts the bank's timeline and RBI complaint route.
  • The tax notice and the income-tax portal "e-proceedings" page
    Every reply starts here.
  • Annual Information Statement (AIS), Form 26AS and your filed returns
    Most notices come from mismatches.
  • Proof of days spent in India and abroad (passport stamps, travel history)
    Decides how you are taxed.
  • Tax residency certificate from your country of residence
    Needed to claim lower tax under a tax treaty.
