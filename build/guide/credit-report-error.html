My credit score or CIBIL report is wrong

Most errors come from the lender's reporting. Correction needs the right proof sent to the right place.

What the law says

Credit information companies and lenders must correct errors within set timelines, and RBI rules provide ₹100 per day compensation for delay beyond 30 days.

Papers that usually help

  • Your latest credit report (all pages)
    Shows exactly what is reported.
  • No-dues / closure letter for the loan or card
    Main proof for wrong active or overdue status.
  • Statements showing EMIs paid on time
    Answers wrong late-payment entries.
  • Settlement letter and payment proof
    Shows whether "settled" is correct.
  • ID and PAN copies, and a police/cyber complaint if someone used your identity
    Identity misuse needs a formal complaint.
  • Dispute reference numbers and replies
    Starts the 30-day clock.
  • Raise an online dispute with the credit bureau and the lender
    Required first step.
