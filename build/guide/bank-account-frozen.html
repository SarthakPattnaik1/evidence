What the law says

Freezes ordered by police, courts or tax authorities follow different procedures. Your advocate will check which authority acted and under what power.

Papers that usually help

  • The freeze message or notice
    It usually names the authority or reason. This decides who can lift the freeze.
  • Your written request to the bank asking for the reason
    Without a written reason, it is hard to know whether to approach the bank, the police or a court.
  • Account statement from about 6 months before the freeze to today
    Your advocate can see the credits that may be linked and the balance that is held.
  • Details of the specific incoming payments (date, amount, UTR/reference, sender)
    Freezes linked to cyber complaints usually point to particular credits.
  • Proof of the deal behind those payments
    Invoices, chats with the buyer, or a trading platform order record can show why the money came to you.
  • Police notice, FIR number or cyber-complaint reference
    Your advocate needs the case details to approach the police or the court.
  • Copy of the court, tax or government order
    Orders have their own appeal or review routes and time limits.
  • The KYC documents you submitted and the bank's acknowledgement
    Shows you complied with the bank's request.
  • All messages and letters with the bank about the freeze
    Creates a timeline and shows whether the bank responded properly.
  • Bank complaint reference number and replies
    Usually needed before going to the RBI complaint system.
  • Proof that you depend on the account (salary slips, business invoices, medical bills)
    Can support a request to release part of the money.
  • Proof that you own the account (passbook, account opening papers, ID)
    Basic identification for any application.

Questions Pehlo will ask you

  • How did you find out the account was frozen?
  • What reason have you been given?
  • Do you have any written message or notice about the restriction?
  • Is the whole account blocked, or only a part of the money?
  • Before the freeze, did you receive money from someone you did not know well?
  • Have the police given you anything?
  • Have you already submitted your KYC documents again?
  • Do you need this account for any of these?
