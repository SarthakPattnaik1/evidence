Papers that usually help

  • Marriage certificate
    Basic proof of the marriage.
  • Other proof of the marriage (wedding photos, invitation card, ceremony receipts, joint documents)
    Used when there is no certificate.
  • ID and address proof for both of you
    Residence affects which court can hear the case.
  • Any written settlement or draft terms
    Mutual-consent petitions record the agreed terms.
  • Proof of the date you started living apart (rent agreement, letters, messages)
    Separation periods can matter.
  • Children's birth certificates, school and medical records
    Needed for custody and support.
  • Records of who takes care of the children day to day
    Courts focus on the child's welfare.
  • Your own income proof (salary slips, ITR, bank statements)
    Maintenance considers both sides. Courts often require an affidavit of income and assets.
  • Lawfully held records of your spouse's income or assets
    Supports a maintenance claim.
  • List of monthly expenses with bills (rent, school, medical, household)
    Maintenance is based on reasonable needs.
  • List of stridhan, jewellery and gifts, with photos or bills
    Supports a claim to return them.
  • Property papers for jointly owned property and loan records
    Needed to divide or protect property.
  • All notices, complaints, court papers and orders received
    Existing cases have deadlines.
  • The petition or notice your spouse sent, with the date you received it
    Response deadlines run from receipt.
