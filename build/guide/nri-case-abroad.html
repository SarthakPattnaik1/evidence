What the law says

Many courts allow exemption from personal appearance and evidence by video conference. Criminal cases may need personal appearance at certain stages. A power-of-attorney holder can file and manage civil cases but cannot testify to facts only you know.

Papers that usually help

  • The notice/summons/warrant and its envelope or email, with date received
    Dates run from receipt.
  • Case status printout (eCourts) with next date
    Avoids missing dates.
  • Signed vakalatnama for your advocate (executed abroad as required)
    Your advocate can't appear without it.
  • Specific PoA for a trusted person to manage the case (civil matters)
    Lets the case move while you're abroad.
  • Proof of residence and job abroad; travel constraints
    Supports exemption from appearance / video evidence.
  • All documents about the underlying dispute
    Your advocate needs them before the first date.
  • Passport and OCI copies
    Needed for affidavits and bail papers.
