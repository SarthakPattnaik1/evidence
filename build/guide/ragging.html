Ragging, bullying or harassment at college or hostel

Ragging is a crime and colleges must act on it. You can complain straight to the national helpline — you do not have to go through your college first.

Answer a few questions and get your list →

What the law says

UGC anti-ragging regulations require every college to have an Anti-Ragging Committee and Squad and to act on complaints. The National Anti-Ragging Helpline (1800-180-5522, 24x7) forwards complaints to the college, university and police. Serious cases are also crimes under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita.

Papers that usually help

  • Dated notes: what happened, where, who was there
    Committees decide on specific incidents.
  • Group chat messages, voice notes, videos
    Hard to deny.
  • Names of witnesses and friends you told
    Committees speak to witnesses.
  • Medical record of any injury
    Needed for a police case.
  • Complaint on 1800-180-5522 or antiragging.in
    Sent to the college, university and police; can be confidential.
