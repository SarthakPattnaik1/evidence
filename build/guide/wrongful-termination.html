What the law says

The four Labour Codes came into force on 21 November 2025. "Workers" have protection against unfair termination; managerial staff above set thresholds may need to rely on their contract instead.

Papers that usually help

  • Appointment letter, contract and any later revision letters
    Defines what you were owed.
  • Termination / resignation-acceptance letter, and the emails around it
    The central document.
  • Messages or recordings showing pressure to resign
    Forced resignation can be treated as termination.
  • Show-cause notice, your reply, inquiry report
    Dismissal without a fair inquiry may be challenged.
  • Appraisals, ratings, appreciation emails, targets met
    Answers performance-based termination.
  • Salary slips, Form 16 and bank statements
    Needed to calculate dues.
  • EPFO passbook / UAN details
    Shows employment and PF dues.
  • Leave balance record
    Calculates encashment.
  • Written request for full-and-final settlement, relieving and experience letters
    Creates a record.
  • Your earlier complaint, leave application or maternity request, and the dates
    Timing can show retaliation.
