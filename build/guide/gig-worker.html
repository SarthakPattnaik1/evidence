What the law says

The Code on Social Security, 2020 (in force from 21 November 2025) recognises gig and platform workers and provides for registration on e-Shram. Rajasthan, Karnataka and some other states have their own gig-worker laws with welfare boards and, in Karnataka, protection against arbitrary deactivation.

Papers that usually help

  • Partner ID, profile screenshots and onboarding agreement
    Basic proof.
  • Trip/order history and payout statements
    Shows unpaid amounts.
  • The blocking message and any reason given
    Needed to challenge it.
  • Your support tickets and replies
    Shows the platform had a chance to fix it.
  • Medical records, FIR (if road accident), and the order you were on
    Needed for insurance and compensation.
  • Register on e-Shram
    Opens social security schemes for platform workers.
