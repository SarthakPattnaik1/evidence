Telecom complaints go first to the company and its appellate authority. The Supreme Court has confirmed you can also go to the Consumer Commission.

What the law says

Every operator must run a complaint centre and an appellate authority under TRAI rules. In Vodafone Idea v. Ajay Kumar Agarwal (2022) the Supreme Court held consumer complaints against telecom companies are maintainable.

Papers that usually help

  • Bills or recharge history for the disputed months
    Shows the wrong charges.
  • Your plan details as sold to you
    Charges outside the plan are disputable.
  • Dates and duration of outages, speed tests, technician visit records
    Supports refund or compensation.
  • Complaint docket numbers and replies
    Needed for escalation.
  • Messages about SIM change, and bank alerts after it
    SIM swap is often followed by bank fraud.
  • Numbers and screenshots of spam/fraud calls
    For reporting and blocking.
  • Raise a complaint and get a docket number
    Starts the process.
