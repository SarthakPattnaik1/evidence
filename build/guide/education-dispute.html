School, college or coaching — fee refund, certificates withheld, or admission/result problem

Colleges must refund fees on withdrawal as per UGC rules and cannot keep your original certificates. Schools and coaching centres have their own rules.

Answer a few questions and get your list →

What the law says

UGC's fee-refund rules require refunds on withdrawal of admission (full refund, with a small processing deduction, if you withdraw in time) and prohibit higher-education institutions from retaining original certificates. School fee increases are regulated by state fee laws in many states. Education services can also be challenged before the Consumer Commission in some cases.

Papers that usually help

  • Fee receipts and payment proof
    Basis of the refund.
  • Student ID / enrolment or roll number and admission records
    Identifies you in any complaint.
  • Admit card, result notice and any reason given for withholding
    Shows what is held back and why.
  • Admission letter, prospectus and fee/refund policy
    Shows the refund and fee terms.
  • Withdrawal application with received stamp/date
    Decides the refund percentage.
  • List of originals submitted and any receipt for them
    Proves retention.
  • Fee hike circulars and parent association communications
    Shows whether the hike followed state rules.
  • Written request and any reply
    Needed for UGC / regulator / court.
  • Complain on UGC e-Samadhaan
    UGC acts on refund and certificate complaints.
