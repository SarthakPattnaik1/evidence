Papers that usually help

  • Rent agreement (all pages)
    Usually states the deposit, notice period and deductions.
  • Any messages or receipts that mention the deposit amount
    Without an agreement, messages and receipts prove what was agreed.
  • Proof of paying the deposit
    The central fact of the claim.
  • Cash receipt, acknowledgement message, or names of witnesses
    Cash is harder to prove; any written acknowledgement helps.
  • Rent payment records for the whole stay
    Answers claims of unpaid rent.
  • Proof of handing back the keys and the date
    Fixes when the deposit became due.
  • Your notice to vacate
    Short notice is a common reason for deductions.
  • Photos or videos of the home's condition
    The main evidence on damage claims.
  • Final electricity, water and maintenance bills and meter readings
    Answers claims of unpaid bills.
  • The messages in which the deposit was refused or deductions claimed
    Shows the reason and its date.
  • Your written demand and any reply
    Fixes the date of demand.
  • Landlord's and tenant's full names and addresses
    Needed for any notice or complaint.
  • Repair invoices and damage photos (landlord)
    Supports deductions.

Questions Pehlo will ask you

  • Which side are you on?

  • How was the deposit paid?
  • Has the house been vacated and the keys handed back?
  • What reason has been given for keeping the deposit?
  • Are there photos or videos of the home's condition?
  • Are any rent or bills unpaid?
