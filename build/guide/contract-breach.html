What the law says

Commercial disputes of ₹3 lakh or more go to commercial courts, generally after mandatory pre-institution mediation unless urgent interim relief is needed. If the contract has an arbitration clause, courts usually refer the dispute to arbitration but can grant interim protection.

Papers that usually help

  • Signed contract with annexures, amendments and SOWs
    The foundation.
  • Emails, quotations, POs and messages forming the deal
    A contract can be formed by correspondence.
  • Delivery records, milestones, acceptance and rejection notes
    Central to breach.
  • Payment records and invoices
    Shows the financial position.
  • Notices of breach, termination and replies
    Fixes timeline and cure periods.
  • Loss calculation with supporting bills, lost orders, replacement costs
    Courts and tribunals award proven loss.
  • Bank guarantee and invocation letter
    Urgent injunctions turn on exact wording.
  • NDA/confidentiality clauses and proof of misuse
    Supports injunction claims.
  • Steps you took to limit the loss
    Required for damages.
