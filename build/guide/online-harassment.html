What the law says

Online harassment can be an offence under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita and the IT Act. Platforms must act on complaints, and must remove intimate or morphed images within 24 hours of a complaint.

Papers that usually help

  • Screenshots showing the content, profile name/URL, date and time
    Evidence before it is deleted.
  • List of links (URLs), usernames and phone numbers involved
    Platforms and police need exact links.
  • Full chat export and call log
    Shows frequency and threats.
  • Payment demands and any payments already made
    Supports extortion charges.
  • Platform report confirmations and cybercrime complaint number
    Needed to escalate.
  • Report the content to the platform and at cybercrime.gov.in
    Starts the takedown clock.
  • Proof of your identity and your genuine profile
    Needed for impersonation takedowns.
