What the law says

Partnership firms are governed by the Partnership Act, 1932; LLPs by the LLP Act, 2008. An unregistered firm faces limits on suing third parties and partners in some cases. Many deeds require arbitration.

Papers that usually help

  • Partnership deed / LLP agreement and amendments
    The starting point.
  • Registration certificate (Form A / LLP incorporation)
    Affects remedies.
  • Proof of capital contributed by each partner
    Basis for return of capital.
  • Books of accounts, bank statements and tax returns of the business
    Shows diversion and profit shares.
  • Evidence of diverted clients or money (invoices raised elsewhere, messages)
    Supports recovery and criminal complaints.
  • Messages, payments and witnesses showing the partnership existed
    Needed when nothing was written.
  • Your notice of retirement/dissolution or the notice expelling you
    Fixes dates and rights.
  • Firm loans, guarantees and creditor demands
    Partners can be personally liable in firms.
