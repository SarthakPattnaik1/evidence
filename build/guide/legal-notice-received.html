A legal notice is not a court order. But it often comes before a case, and replying well matters.

What the law says

Most legal notices are demands before a civil or criminal case. Some (like cheque-bounce notices) start statutory clocks.

Papers that usually help

  • The notice and its envelope (keep the postal stamp)
    The date of receipt matters.
  • All documents about the underlying deal or relationship
    Needed to reply accurately.
  • Payment records
    Answers money claims.
  • The post or statement complained about, with context
    Defamation depends on exact words and context.
  • A short timeline of the dispute
    Helps draft a precise reply.

Questions Pehlo will ask you

  • What is the notice about?
  • How many days does it give you?
  • When did you receive it?
  • Do you agree with what it claims?
