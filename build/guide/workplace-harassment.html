What the law says

The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013 requires workplaces with 10 or more employees to have an Internal Committee. A Local Committee in each district covers others. Complaints are generally due within 3 months of the last incident (extendable by 3 months).

Papers that usually help

  • A dated, private account of each incident: what happened, where, who was nearby
    Detailed notes made close to the time carry weight.
  • Messages, emails, call logs or voice notes from the person
    Direct evidence.
  • Names of people who saw, heard, or were told at the time
    Supports your account.
  • Your employer's POSH policy and committee details
    Names the committee and process.
  • Your written complaint and acknowledgement
    Fixes the date.
  • Records of retaliation (transfers, bad reviews, exclusion) with dates
    Can be raised with the committee.
