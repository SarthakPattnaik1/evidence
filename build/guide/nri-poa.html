Giving, using or cancelling a power of attorney from abroad

A power of attorney is how most NRIs act in India. A badly drafted or badly executed one causes more disputes than it solves.

What the law says

A PoA signed abroad is generally executed before an Indian consulate, or before a notary and then apostilled. It must usually be stamped in India within a set time after it arrives, and registered if it will be used to sell property.

Papers that usually help

  • A clear PoA draft naming the property/case and the exact powers
    Vague, broad drafts are the main cause of misuse and rejection.
  • Execution proof: consular attestation, or notarisation + apostille certificate
    Registrars and courts check this first.
  • Stamp duty payment / adjudication in India
    Unstamped documents can be refused or impounded.
  • Registration of the PoA (if it will be used to sell property)
    Many registrars require it.
  • Passport/OCI copies of you and ID of the attorney holder; photos
    Required at execution and registration.
  • The written rejection or objection
    Tells you what to fix.
  • Revocation deed executed abroad, and notices to the holder, sub-registrar, banks and buyers
    Revocation must be communicated to be effective against others.
  • Records of what the holder did (deeds, bank withdrawals, messages)
    Supports civil and criminal action.
