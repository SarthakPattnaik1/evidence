What the law says

A foreign divorce is generally recognised in India only if the court had proper jurisdiction and decided on grounds available under the law the couple married under, with both sides participating. India is not a party to the Hague Convention on child abduction, so courts decide custody on the child's welfare.

Papers that usually help

  • Marriage certificate, wedding photos, invitation
    Basic proof in every case.
  • Get the marriage registered (if it isn't)
    Registration makes enforcement and passport-linked remedies easier.
  • Spouse's passport copy, visa/residence details, foreign address, employer
    Needed for service of summons, maintenance enforcement and passport action.
  • Proof of desertion: last contact, travel dates, messages, money stopped
    Supports maintenance, DV and divorce claims.
  • Records of dowry demands, money transferred, stridhan kept
    Supports criminal and recovery claims.
  • The foreign divorce decree/petition and proof of how you were served
    Decides whether India will recognise it.
  • Child's birth certificate, passport details, school records, travel details
    Courts decide custody on the child's welfare and habitual residence.
  • FIR, complaint, summons or orders in India
    Your response starts here.
  • Any Look Out Circular, passport notice or impounding order
    May need urgent legal action before travel.
  • Your residence, job and immigration papers abroad
    Relevant to appearance, bail and jurisdiction.
  • Income and expense records (both spouses, as far as lawfully known)
    Courts require income disclosure.
