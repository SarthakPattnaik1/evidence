What the law says

Hacking and identity theft are offences under the IT Act. Report on cybercrime.gov.in; for money lost call 1930. Platforms must act quickly on impersonation complaints under the IT Rules.

Papers that usually help

  • Your account username, linked email and phone
    Needed for recovery and complaints.
  • Proof it is yours: old screenshots, ID, photos you posted, purchase receipts
    Platforms ask for this during recovery.
  • Security emails / SMS about login, password or email change
    Shows the hack and its timing; often has a "secure account" link.
  • Link to the fake profile and screenshots of it
    Needed for the takedown report.
  • Messages sent from your account to others
    Shows what the hacker did in your name.
  • Use the app's official "hacked account" recovery page
    Only the official flow can restore access.
  • Change password, turn on two-step verification, log out other devices
    Stops the hacker getting back in.
  • Warn your contacts not to send money or OTPs
    Hackers target your friends next.
  • Complaint on cybercrime.gov.in (and 1930 if money was lost)
    Needed if recovery fails or money was taken.
