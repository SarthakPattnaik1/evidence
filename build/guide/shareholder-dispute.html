What the law says

Oppression and mismanagement cases go to the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT). Members generally need at least 10% of shares (or 100 members), though the Tribunal can waive this. Shareholders' agreements often add arbitration.

Papers that usually help

  • Memorandum and Articles of Association (current version)
    Sets transfer, appointment and meeting rules.
  • Shareholders' / founders' agreement and term sheets
    Often decides exit, vesting and deadlock.
  • Share certificates, register of members extract, cap table, allotment filings
    Proves your stake and any dilution.
  • MCA filings: director appointments/resignations, allotments, charges, annual returns
    Shows unauthorised changes and their dates.
  • Board and general meeting notices, agendas and minutes you hold
    Defective notice or quorum can invalidate decisions.
  • Financial statements, bank statements and related-party transactions you can lawfully access
    Supports mismanagement claims.
  • Your written requests for records/meetings and the replies
    Shows exclusion from management.
  • Founder messages and emails about roles, equity and exits
    Fills gaps where nothing was signed.
  • Latest valuation reports and funding round documents
    Needed for any buy-out.
