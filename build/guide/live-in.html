Live-in relationship — rights, breakup, money, or family pressure

Living together is legal for adults. Long, marriage-like relationships also bring some protection — especially for women and children.

Answer a few questions and get your list →

What the law says

Live-in relationships between adults are protected by the right to life and liberty. Women in a relationship "in the nature of marriage" can seek protection, residence and money under the Domestic Violence Act, 2005, and maintenance under the BNSS. Children are treated as legitimate. Uttarakhand requires live-in couples to register under its Uniform Civil Code (since January 2025).

Papers that usually help

  • Proof you lived together: rent agreement, shared address on IDs, bills, deliveries
    The starting point for any claim.
  • Photos, trips, social media, people who knew you as a couple
    Shows a relationship "in the nature of marriage".
  • Bank transfers, joint purchases, who paid for what
    Supports claims for money or belongings.
  • Child's birth certificate and school records
    Needed for child support.
  • Threat messages and names of those threatening
    Supports police or court protection.
  • Medical records, photos, messages showing abuse
    Needed for protection orders.
  • Age and ID proof of both
    Police and courts check this first.
