What the law says

DGCA's revised refund rules (in force from 26 March 2026): refunds within 14 working days, the airline is responsible even when you booked through an agent or portal, a free 48-hour "look-in" to cancel or change (for bookings made well ahead), and free name correction within 24 hours on direct bookings. Other travel disputes go to the National Consumer Helpline and Consumer Commission.

Papers that usually help

  • Ticket / booking confirmation and fare breakup
    Basis for any refund.
  • Payment proof
    Refund is calculated from it.
  • Cancellation, delay or schedule-change message from them
    Decides whether you get a full refund or compensation.
  • Your cancellation confirmation and the refund amount shown
    Shows deductions charged.
  • Baggage irregularity report (PIR) and baggage tag
    Needed for any baggage claim.
  • Boarding pass and proof you reached in time
    Needed for denied boarding or delay claims.
  • Brochure, itinerary or promises made
    Proves service fell short.
  • Written complaint and its reference number
    Starts formal timelines.
  • Complain on AirSewa
    Ministry portal that airlines respond to.
  • File on National Consumer Helpline (1915)
    Travel is one of its biggest refund sectors.
