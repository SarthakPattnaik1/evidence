What the law says

IT Rules (amended, in force from 20 February 2026): platforms must remove non-consensual intimate images, including AI-made or morphed ones, within 2 hours of a complaint. Sharing such images is a crime under the IT Act and the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita. Report on cybercrime.gov.in (women and child safety section) or 1930.

Papers that usually help

  • Links (URLs) and usernames where it is posted or sent from
    Platforms remove by link; police trace accounts by username.
  • Screenshots of threats and demands, with date and time
    Proves the crime even if they delete it.
  • Any payments you made to them
    Helps trace them and freeze accounts.
  • An original photo of you that was used (for fakes)
    Shows the morph or deepfake.
  • Report to the platform (choose "nudity / non-consensual / impersonation")
    They must act within 2 hours for this kind of content.
  • Complaint on cybercrime.gov.in or at the police station
    Starts investigation and lets police order takedowns.
  • Use StopNCII.org to block the image on partner platforms
    Works without sending the actual image.
  • Stop replying and do not pay
    Paying almost always leads to more demands.
