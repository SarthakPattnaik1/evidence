What the law says

Stray dogs are managed by municipal bodies under the Animal Birth Control Rules, 2023. In the ongoing Supreme Court case on stray dogs (since July 2025), the Court in January 2026 warned that it would make state governments pay heavy compensation for dog-bite injuries and deaths. Some High Courts have already fixed compensation formulas. A pet owner who negligently fails to control an animal can be liable under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 and must pay damages.

Papers that usually help

  • Hospital records, vaccine card and bills
    Basis of any compensation.
  • Photos of wounds (with a scale or ruler) taken the same day
    Some courts fix compensation per tooth mark and wound size.
  • Location details and photos/video of the animal or area
    Identifies the responsible body.
  • Pet owner’s name and address, and any earlier complaints
    Owner liability.
  • Written complaint to the municipality (and any earlier complaints about the same dogs)
    Supports a claim against the authority.
  • Police complaint (for a pet attack or a death)
    Needed for a criminal case or claim.
  • Proof of lost income or school days
    Adds to compensation.
