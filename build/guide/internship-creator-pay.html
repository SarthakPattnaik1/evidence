What you can claim depends on what was written down. Even emails and DMs count as a contract.

What the law says

A promise of payment in an email, offer letter or chat can be enforced as a contract (Indian Contract Act). Unpaid amounts can be demanded by legal notice and recovered through a civil suit or, for some cases, the consumer or labour route. Influencers must disclose paid promotions under ASCI and consumer rules.

Papers that usually help

  • Offer letter, contract, or the emails/DMs where pay was agreed
    The core of your claim.
  • Proof of work delivered: files, posts, links, approval messages
    Shows they accepted it.
  • Invoices or payment requests you sent and any replies
    Shows the amount and the delay.
  • Attendance, ID card, work emails, certificates
    Proves the internship.
  • The revocation or delay message, and what you gave up (other offers, resignation)
    Supports a claim for losses.
  • Payment proof for the fee and the advert that promised a job
    Often a scam — basis for refund or complaint.
  • Send a polite written demand with a deadline
    Many pay at this stage; it is also needed before a case.

Questions Saboot will ask you

  • What is it about?
  • What do you have in writing?
  • Did you complete the work or period?
  • How much is owed?
  • When was the payment due?
