What the law says

Under the RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, you must first complain to the bank. If there is no reply in 30 days, or the reply is unsatisfactory, you can complain on cms.rbi.org.in — generally within one year of the bank's reply.

Papers that usually help

  • Account statement showing the issue
    Shows charges, debits or restrictions.
  • Account number, branch and customer ID
    Every complaint form asks for it.
  • A short dated timeline of what happened
    Makes your complaint clear and complete.
  • Bank's schedule of charges or product terms
    Charges outside it are disputable.
  • Policy document, proposal form and premium debits
    Mis-selling turns on what you were told and signed.
  • Messages or notes of what the staff told you
    Shows you were misled.
  • Any notice of closure or restriction
    Banks must give notice and reasons.
  • Locker agreement, rent receipts, and inventory if any
    Decides bank liability.
  • Written complaint to the bank and its reply
    Required before RBI.
  • Send a written complaint to the bank (email or grievance portal)
    Starts the 30-day clock.
  • File on cms.rbi.org.in
    Free and binding on the bank if you accept the award.
