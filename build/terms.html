1What Saboot is

Saboot is a free tool that asks simple questions and gives you a list of papers and facts to take to an advocate, in your language.

2Not legal advice

Saboot gives general information. It does not give legal advice, does not tell you what a court will decide, and does not say whether a document will be accepted. Using Saboot does not create an advocate–client relationship.

3Accuracy and updates

Each guide is prepared from official sources and shows the date its law was last checked. Guides marked Draft have not yet been reviewed by an advocate. Laws, deadlines and fees can change, so confirm with an advocate before you act.

4Using Saboot fairly

  • Use Saboot only for lawful purposes.
  • Do not try to obtain other people’s records without lawful permission.
  • Do not misuse the service or try to disrupt it.

5Advocates

Saboot does not recommend, rank or advertise any advocate. Choose any advocate you trust. Free legal aid is available from your Legal Services Authority — call 15100.

6Links to other websites

Guides link to official websites (for example cybercrime.gov.in or e-Jagriti). We are not responsible for their content or availability.

7Limits of responsibility

Saboot is provided free and “as is”. To the extent the law allows, its makers are not responsible for any loss arising from reliance on it.
