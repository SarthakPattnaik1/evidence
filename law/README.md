# Law sources

Official texts Saboot's time limits are built from. Nothing here is typed from memory.

| File | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `limitation-act-1963.txt` | Full text of the Limitation Act, 1963 (sections 1–32 and the Schedule, as amended up to 2019) | Tripura High Court's copy of the official text, fetched 31 Oct 2026 by the `law-sources` branch workflow; PDF and SHA-256 kept on that branch |
| `limitation-schedule.json` | All 137 articles of the Schedule: description, period, when the period starts, and the raw printed text | Parsed from the file above |

Every time limit shown in the app must cite an article here (or a section of a special law added the same way), and be signed off by an advocate before it goes live.
